## 3. The Wiro.ae Signature Scroll & Design System

### 3.1 Core Visual Elements
1. **Fixed Dead-Center Zoom Portal (`<AgencyChaosIntro>`):**
   - Locked directly to `fixed inset-0` with custom wheel and touch gesture tracking. The window **never scrolls upward**—it zooms symmetrically from the dead center of the screen directly toward the viewer's face!
2. **Day-to-Night Sky Transition & StarField:**
   - As you zoom into the door, the daytime blue sky smoothly transitions into deep evening indigo, and then dark midnight obsidian.
   - Fluffy white cumulus clouds dissolve away and are replaced by a galaxy of twinkling golden stars!
3. **Girl Sipping Coffee in the Chaotic Office Scene:**
   - Features the girl in a black blazer and sunglasses calmly sipping coffee while the office and computer monitors burn behind her, beside the cardboard box labeled `BRAND / CONTENT / STRATEGY / CAMPAIGNS` and the concrete pillar with the `PUNE` sign.
4. **Brand Name in Solid Crisp White:**
   - Single-colour solid white title (`text-white font-serif font-black`) with classical drop shadow.
5. **The Gilded Baroque Picture Frame & Agency OS:**
   - Multi-layered antique gold frame with carved moulding, rococo filigree corners, and gold crest wrapping the live desktop OS.
   - Houses the full Agency OS with left sidebar, 4 pastel metric cards (Lime, Pink with "Approve" button, Sage WhatsApp, Cyan Pipeline ₹71.2L), and "Projects in Motion" displaying the 3 uploaded client photos.
6. **Fifteen Modules with Classical Sky Backdrop:**
   - Section headline: *"One system for every page your agency runs"*
   - Subtitle: *"Fifteen modules, one shared record of the work. Nothing lives in its own silo."*
   - Serene Renaissance Pegasus and collage clouds backdrop with expandable pink `+` buttons.

## 4. Verification & Deployment Instructions

The master file [App.tsx](file:///c:/Users/vedan/.gemini/antigravity/scratch/acesutra-website/App.tsx) is completely self-contained with zero external dependencies or broken paths:
1. Open your repository on GitHub (`https://github.com/AceSutra/acesutra-Web`).
2. Navigate to `src/App.tsx` and click **Edit**.
3. Paste the contents of `App.tsx` and click **Commit changes**.
4. Vercel deploys with 0 warnings in strict TypeScript mode.
